## SCORING DIMENSIONS

1. **premise_strength** (1-5): Does this campaign have a genuine dramatic premise that creates pressure, not just a setting and plot?
   - 1: No discernible premise beyond "things happen in Star Wars"
   - 3: Has a premise but it's familiar or doesn't create genuine pressure
   - 5: Sharp, specific premise that creates a genuine dilemma

2. **npc_thematic_diversity** (1-5): Do NPCs embody different perspectives or answers to the campaign's themes?
   - 1: NPCs are functionally interchangeable or serve only plot roles
   - 3: NPCs have distinct motivations but don't create thematic tension
   - 5: Major NPCs embody competing worldviews relevant to the throughline

3. **dramatic_progression** (1-5): Does the act structure build tension through causally connected escalation?
   - 1: Acts feel episodic with no causal chain
   - 3: Acts connect but escalation is mechanical (just bigger threats)
   - 5: Each act's pressure flows from previous consequences

4. **throughline_testability** (1-5): Is the throughline question meaningfully tested through gameplay?
   - 1: Throughline is decorative — never tested in play
   - 3: Throughline is testable in one act or through one NPC
   - 5: Throughline is tested from multiple angles across multiple acts

5. **anti_genericity** (1-5): Does this campaign resist generic Star Wars patterns?
   - 1: Standard hero vs. villain, light vs. dark, redemption arc
   - 3: Avoids the worst cliches but still feels familiar
   - 5: Genuinely distinctive — could not be a different campaign
