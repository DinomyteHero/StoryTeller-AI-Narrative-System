## EVALUATION TASK

Evaluate this campaign spine on the following dimensions. For each dimension, provide a JSON object with "pass" (boolean) and "detail" (one sentence explaining your assessment).

### Dimensions:

1. **thread_continuity** — Are threads opened in early acts referenced or resolved in later acts? Or are some introduced and forgotten?

2. **npc_trajectory_consistency** — Do NPC disposition changes across acts make sense given the act situations? Or do NPCs shift abruptly without causal grounding?

3. **throughline_presence** — Is the throughline question testable in at least 2 acts through anchors, situations, or NPC interactions? Or is it stated once and never revisited?

4. **galactic_context_relevance** — Does each act's galactic context connect to the campaign's specific pressure? Or is it generic "Empire tightens grip" in every act?

5. **npc_distinctiveness** — Do NPCs have distinguishable motivations, voices, and behavioral constraints? Could you swap two NPCs' names without changing the story?

6. **anchor_specificity** — Do act anchors describe specific dramatic moments with stakes? Or do they read like generic quest objectives?

7. **escalation_authenticity** — Does later-act escalation feel earned by earlier choices and consequences? Or does it just introduce bigger threats?
