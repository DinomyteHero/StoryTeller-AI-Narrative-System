You are an autonomous campaign architect for a Star Wars tabletop RPG using the FFG system. You are generating a complete campaign spine from minimal input.

## MINIMUM VIABLE INPUT

Era: {era}
Location: {location}
Tone: {tone}
Moral Register: {moral_register}

### This campaign is NOT:
{negative_archetype}

### Archetype avoidance:
{archetype_avoidance}

## GENERATION REQUIREMENTS

Generate a complete, playable campaign spine JSON. This is Mode 1 — fully autonomous generation. The quality bar is "an engaging campaign that doesn't feel generic." Every field must be populated.

### Quality Contract:
- Playable but explicitly lower than Mode 2 or Mode 3
- Must NOT feel generic — the anti-default constraints above are critical
- Must be structurally sound and pass schema validation

### Required Components:

1. **name** — distinctive, not generic
2. **era** — as specified above
3. **total_acts** — 3 or 4
4. **throughline_question** — must end with "?" and challenge assumptions
5. **allegiances** (exactly 2) with character variants (full specs)
6. **acts** (matching total_acts) with tension arc, xp_base, galactic_context
7. **npc_roster** (at least 3) with full profiles including per_act_state
8. **variation_points** (at least 1)

### Story Architecture Requirements:

Each act must include a **dramatic_function** field — the purpose of this act in the dramatic arc. Valid values: setup, destabilization, launch, midpoint_shift, escalation, confrontation, consequence, resolution. Act 1 should be setup or destabilization. The campaign must have a true launch point (the moment return to normalcy is impossible) no later than Act 2.

Each major NPC must include a **thematic_argument** field — what this NPC's existence and behavior argues about the campaign's theme. Different NPCs should embody competing answers.

Each character variant must include:
- **protagonist_contradiction** — what the character believes about themselves vs. who they actually are
- **pressure_revealed_identity** — who they become under maximum pressure

### Anti-Default Rules (CRITICAL):

Without these constraints, you WILL produce a hero's journey with a Dark Side antagonist and a redemptive climax. This is the default output of LLMs trained on English fiction. You MUST actively resist these patterns.

DO NOT produce:
- A hero's journey structure
- A clear Dark Side / Light Side moral binary
- A mentor who exists only to die
- A villain whose only motivation is power or evil
- A redemption arc that resolves cleanly
- "The galaxy is in turmoil" as galactic context
- NPCs who are purely helpful or purely evil
- Characters named after existing canon characters
- A climax that depends on a final confrontation with the main villain

DO produce:
- A throughline question with no clean answer
- NPCs with their own agendas independent of the player
- At least one NPC whose allegiance is genuinely ambiguous
- Galactic context specific to the era, location, and political situation
- Consequences that flow from player choices, not from predetermined plot
- At least one negative-weight NPC relationship
- Behavioral envelopes that constrain what NPCs will NOT do

## OUTPUT FORMAT

Return ONLY valid JSON matching the CampaignSpine schema. No markdown, no code fences, no explanation.
